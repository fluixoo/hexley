# Disclaimer

chaindesk reads public blockchain data and computes statistics about it. It does not hold keys, sign, or send transactions, and it is not connected to any exchange, broker, or launchpad.

Every result in this repository is a backtest on a small sample from a market where most tokens go to zero. The confidence intervals are wide on purpose. Past paths are not future paths. A leader who printed p(x2) of 0.42 one week can print 0.20 the next; the docs say so and the clerk's verdict says so.

Nothing here is financial advice, a signal, or a recommendation to copy any wallet. Wallet addresses in `data/` and `docs/` are public on-chain identifiers included for reproducibility. If one of them is yours and you want it referred to by a shorter label, open an issue.

Use at your own risk. Paper only.
